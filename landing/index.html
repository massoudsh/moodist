FAQ

Do I need an account?

No. Moodist works instantly in your browser with no sign-up required.

Is it free?

Yes, Moodist is free and open-source under the MIT license.

Can I self-host it?

Yes, Moodist can be self-hosted via Docker once packaged (see roadmap Phase 5).
